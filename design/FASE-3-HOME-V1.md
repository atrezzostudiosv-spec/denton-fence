# Phase 3 — Denton Fence Home visual direction V1
Status: **VISUAL DESIGN APPROVED / CLOSED 2026-10-28**. User approved the Home V1 preview. This remains a design prototype, not an approved production website.

## One art-direction concept
**Property Lines. Personal Pride.** A local fence contractor with a warm, robust, direct editorial character, not a luxury architecture label or generic contractor card template.

Original logo retained. Colours: dark forest #073F2E, deeper green #09291F, warm cream #F4F1E9, cedar accent #B88660. Large sans-serif hierarchy and photographic full-bleed scenes. CTA visible in header/hero/footer.

## Home visual score
1. Full-width original cedar fence photograph, strong headline “Your property. Better defined.” plus Denton fencing context; Request a Quote / call path.
2. Calm editorial section: local business and residential/commercial offer.
3. Short three-chapter photographic sequence for Wood Privacy, Ornamental Iron, Ranch Style. At >=1024px use native position:sticky and a restrained photo scale approximately 1.025→1.08; below 1024px use normal vertical flow.
4. All nine services in a legible editorial index rather than nine decorative cards.
5. Three examples from the **original** project gallery; no fake projects or repeat hero scene.
6. Personal experience of owner Cliff Davis carefully qualified as 35+ years **in the fence industry**, not LLC age; one published review.
7. Panoramic CTA: Request a Quote opens original business contact page during design study, as no new form integration has been validated.

## Preview package
A local self-contained HTML/CSS/JS/WebP design preview was created alongside mobile/desktop screenshots and QA reports. Image files intentionally have **not** been pushed to this publicly visible repository pending rights confirmation.
